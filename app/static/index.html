1. Prepare your capture

Print 15 cm marker ↗

A printed marker is required in both photos. Print the supplied marker at 100% and measure its black outer square with a ruler: exactly 15 × 15 cm. Place it flat, upright, facing the camera and beside the body—not on a wall behind it.

Front photo

  • Face the camera and stand straight.
  • Include the whole head and both feet, with a little space around them.
  • Remove shoes and wear fitted clothing.
  • Keep arms slightly away from your torso.

Side photo

  • Turn fully sideways and stand straight.
  • Keep your arms clear of the waist outline.
  • Show the entire body and marker again.
  • Use a similar distance and a level camera at waist/chest height.

Only one person in frame. Use a plain background and good lighting. JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 15 MB each. For iPhone HEIC photos, export as JPEG or choose “Most Compatible” in camera settings.
